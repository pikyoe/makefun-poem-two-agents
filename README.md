A poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — and two agents agreeing on one plan. Two minds face one door; they don't ask the wind which way to lean, they choose, they step, and two hands turn one latch together.

## The poem

> Two minds, one door.
> One: the road is wide.
> The other: ours.
>
> We don't ask the wind
> which way to lean.
> We choose. We step.
>
> Agency is no gift.
> Two hands, one latch.
>
> One plan. One turn.
> The door opens.
